Olist Customer Satisfaction Analysis

An in-depth analysis to identify the key drivers of negative customer reviews and provide actionable strategies for improving the customer experience.

By Priyangshu Mukherjee

The Business Problem

Olist, one of Brazil's largest e-commerce platforms, connects small businesses to a wider marketplace. Despite its success, internal reports have indicated a concerning rise in poor customer satisfaction. This analysis aims to dissect Olist's vast dataset to uncover the primary reasons behind negative customer reviews. The central business question is: “What are the key drivers of negative reviews, and how can we use these insights to improve the overall customer experience?”

FINDING 1

Late Delivery is the Main Driver of Negative Reviews

The analysis overwhelmingly points to one primary factor driving negative reviews: delivery delays. While not all negative reviews are due to late deliveries, a significant delay dramatically increases the likelihood of customer dissatisfaction. The two plots below explore this relationship in detail.

Delivery Delay Density Profiles

This density plot clearly shows that negative reviews (orange) spike sharply for orders delivered late (right of the zero-line). Conversely, positive reviews (dark grey) are concentrated among orders delivered on time or early.

Delivery vs. Seller Processing Time

This scatter plot visualizes individual orders. The heavy concentration of orange dots on the right side confirms that late deliveries, regardless of seller processing time, are strongly correlated with negative reviews.

FINDING 2

Certain Product Categories Consistently Underperform

While delivery is a major factor, it doesn't account for all negative reviews. This chart displays the top 20 product categories with the highest percentage of negative reviews. Categories like "Bed, Bath & Table" and "Health & Beauty" stand out, suggesting potential issues with product quality, damage during shipping, or inaccurate descriptions that lead to customer dissatisfaction independent of delivery performance.

FINDING 3

Southeastern Brazil is a Hotspot for Negative Reviews

This bubble map visualizes seller locations across Brazil. The size of each bubble represents the total number of reviews from that location, while the color (from green to red) indicates the percentage of those reviews that are negative. A clear concentration of larger, red-hued bubbles is visible in the populous southeastern states like São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro, highlighting regional disparities in customer satisfaction that are likely tied to local logistics challenges.

Actionable Recommendations

1. Launch a Tiered Seller Performance Program Focused on Shipping Speed.

Why: The data proves that shipping delays are the single biggest driver of customer dissatisfaction. By incentivizing sellers to reduce their processing time, Olist can directly address the root cause of many negative reviews.

How: Establish clear KPIs for seller processing time; offer incentives like “fast shipper” badges or commission discounts; and implement a warning system for consistently slow sellers.

2. Conduct a Quality Audit on the Lowest-Performing Product Categories.

Why: These categories underperform even when delivery times are accounted for, suggesting deeper issues with product quality or damage. This directly impacts customer trust and Olist’s brand reputation.

How: The Operations team should partner with the Seller Relationship team to investigate sellers in these categories, review product listings for accuracy, and consider stricter packaging requirements.

3. Optimize Logistics Partnerships in Underperforming Geographic Regions.

Why: The geographic analysis indicates that systemic, regional issues are at play, likely related to the performance of specific logistics carriers.

How: Use the performance data to hold current logistics partners accountable in these regions, explore partnerships with new local carriers, and adjust delivery estimates to be more realistic in the short term.
