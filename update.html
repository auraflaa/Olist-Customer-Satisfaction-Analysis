Introduction: Understanding Olist's Customer Satisfaction Challenge

Olist, one of Brazil's largest e-commerce platforms, connects small businesses to a wider marketplace. Despite its success, internal reports have indicated a concerning rise in poor customer satisfaction. This analysis aims to dissect Olist's vast dataset to uncover the primary reasons behind negative customer reviews.

By identifying the key drivers of dissatisfaction—whether they be delivery delays, product quality issues, or regional service disparities—we can provide actionable recommendations to help Olist improve its customer experience, reduce churn, and foster long-term growth.

FINDING 1

Late Delivery is the Main Driver of Negative Reviews

The analysis overwhelmingly points to one primary factor driving negative reviews: delivery delays. While not all negative reviews are due to late deliveries, a significant delay dramatically increases the likelihood of customer dissatisfaction. The two plots below explore this relationship in detail.

Delivery Delay Density Profiles

This density plot clearly shows that negative reviews (orange) spike sharply for orders delivered late (right of the zero-line). Conversely, positive reviews (dark grey) are concentrated among orders delivered on time or early.

Delivery vs. Seller Processing Time

This scatter plot visualizes individual orders. The heavy concentration of orange dots on the right side confirms that late deliveries, regardless of seller processing time, are strongly correlated with negative reviews.

FINDING 2

Certain Product Categories Are More Prone to Negative Feedback

While delivery is a major factor, it doesn't account for all negative reviews. This chart displays the top 20 product categories with the highest percentage of negative reviews. Categories like "Bed, Bath & Table" and "Health & Beauty" stand out, suggesting potential issues with product quality, damage during shipping, or inaccurate descriptions that lead to customer dissatisfaction independent of delivery performance.

FINDING 3

Southeastern Brazil is a Hotspot for Negative Reviews

This bubble map visualizes seller locations across Brazil. The size of each bubble represents the total number of reviews from that location, while the color (from green to red) indicates the percentage of those reviews that are negative. A clear concentration of larger, red-hued bubbles is visible in the populous southeastern states like São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro, highlighting regional disparities in customer satisfaction that are likely tied to local logistics challenges.

Conclusion & Actionable Recommendations

1. Optimize Logistics in the Southeast: The data clearly points to delivery delays, particularly in southeastern Brazil, as the primary source of customer dissatisfaction. Olist should prioritize optimizing its supply chain and setting stricter SLAs with logistics partners in this region.

2. Conduct Quality Audits for Problem Categories: For high-complaint categories like 'cama_mesa_banho', a quality control audit is essential. This could involve stricter seller vetting, improved packaging guidelines, and verifying product descriptions to reduce returns and negative feedback.

3. Empower Sellers with Better Tools: Regional performance differences suggest a need for better seller support. Olist could provide enhanced training and tools focused on best practices for packaging, timely shipping, and customer communication to sellers in lower-scoring states.
